From Maguire’s article / attached model table (read from published images):

  • FY2028 total revenue $731.1B; Adj. EBITDA $536.2B; CapEx $369.0B; change in cash +$114.9B
  • Terrestrial compute EOY GW: 2.5 / 10.2 / 21.0 (FY26–28); Orbital: 0 / 0.15 / 2.8
  • Annual rev/GW stabilizes at $40B; CapEx/GW declines toward $30B by late 2028
  • Starlink ~$20B/yr on ~400 F9 launches; 1 Starship ≈ 20 F9 → ~$1B/yr per flight; ~5 yr life → ~$5B lifetime
  • rev/GW and CapEx/GW are coupled constants; CapEx/GW unlikely <$20B in ~4 years; $10B is a stress case
  • DTC may exceed Starlink by 2029–30; Terafab / orbital compute widen error bars; orbital ramps from 2029 in narrative

Our lens (labeled, adjustable): payback years = CapEx/GW ÷ (rev/GW × margin); compare to GPU life 4–6 yrs. Starlink check: ~$20B 3-yr connectivity CapEx vs ~$7.2B FY25 segment EBITDA ≈ ~2.8 yr simple payback on that stock (≈2 yr per-sat math elsewhere).

Wide error bars. Macro can move rev/GW a lot. This page is a toy for debate, not a forecast.
